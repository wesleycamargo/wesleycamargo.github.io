## 🛡️ [Microsoft October 2026 Patch Tuesday fixes 1,411 flaws, 3 zero-days](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/microsoft/microsoft-october-2026-patch-tuesday-fixes-1-411-flaws-3-zero-days/)

<p class="news-brief-byline">BleepingComputer · Lawrence Abrams · October 13, 2026</p>

<p class="news-brief-lead">Microsoft patched <strong>1,411 vulnerabilities</strong>, including <strong>70 critical flaws</strong> and a Windows issue already exploited in attacks.</p>

Attackers exploited **CVE-2026-96951** to bypass Windows *Mark of the Web* protections.

<p class="news-brief-action"><strong>For defenders:</strong> Prioritize security updates and identify Windows devices that have reached end of support.</p>

<p class="news-brief-topic">Security · Containers</p>

## 🔐 [Docker Hub security advisory: personal access tokens transferred during account-to-organization conversions](https://www.docker.com/ja-jp/blog/docker-hub-security-advisory-cve-2026-105445/)

<p class="news-brief-byline">Docker · Mark Lechner · October 12, 2026</p>

<p class="news-brief-lead">Docker fixed <strong>CVE-2026-105445</strong>, a flaw that could transfer unintended repository access during account-to-organization conversions.</p>

The problem could affect container images and access tokens. Docker applied its fix on **September 30**, notified potentially affected customers, and reported **no evidence of exploitation**.

<p class="news-brief-topic">Netherlands · Semiconductors</p>

## 🇳🇱 [ASML says AI demand will exceed chip-tool capacity through 2028](https://www.reuters.com/business/asml-forecasts-q4-sales-above-expectations-ai-chip-demand-2026-10-14/)

<p class="news-brief-byline">Reuters · Nathan Vifflin and Toby Sterling · October 14, 2026</p>

<p class="news-brief-lead">ASML expects AI-driven demand for chipmaking equipment to exceed its manufacturing capacity in <strong>2027 and 2028</strong>.</p>

Customer orders now stretch into **2030**. ASML forecasts **€14.2–15.2 billion** in fourth-quarter 2026 sales and is considering further expansion.

<p class="news-brief-topic">Science · Artificial intelligence</p>

## 🔬 [Google DeepMind, Google Cloud to allow European researchers access to frontier models](https://www.reuters.com/technology/google-deepmind-google-cloud-allow-european-researchers-access-frontier-models-2026-10-14/)

<p class="news-brief-byline">Reuters · Foo Yun Chee · October 14, 2026</p>

<p class="news-brief-lead">Up to <strong>200 European researchers</strong> will gain access to Google's advanced AI tools for scientific work.</p>

<p class="news-brief-topic">Developer platforms · Identity</p>

## 🔑 [Secret scanning blocks Anthropic keys by default, adds Salesforce](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-10-13-secret-scanning-blocks-anthropic-keys-by-default-adds-salesforce/)

<p class="news-brief-byline">GitHub Changelog · October 13, 2026</p>

<p class="news-brief-lead">GitHub now blocks certain <strong>Anthropic API keys</strong> by default where secret scanning and push protection are enabled.</p>

Its detection coverage also expands to **Salesforce and Cloudsmith** credentials.

<p class="news-brief-action"><strong>For engineering teams:</strong> Check that secret scanning and push protection are enabled on repositories used by AI coding agents.</p>
